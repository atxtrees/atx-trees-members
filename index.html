❓Q&A with Albert
How often should I water a newly planted tree?
For the first 2 weeks, water every day. Then every 2-3 days for the first month. After that, deep water 1-2 times per week through the first summer. The goal is to keep the root ball moist but never waterlogged. Stick your finger 2 inches into the soil — if it's dry, water it.
My tree dropped all its leaves. Is it dead?
Not necessarily! Do the scratch test — scratch a small section of bark on a branch with your fingernail. If you see green underneath, the tree is alive. If it's brown and dry all the way through, that branch may be dead. Try multiple branches before deciding. Call us if you're unsure — (512) 749-5149.
How long before my privacy trees create a full screen?
It depends on the species and size you planted. Nellie R. Stevens Holly grows 3-4 feet per year. Eastern Red Cedar grows 1-2 feet per year. If you planted 65-gallon trees, you'll have significant screening in 2-3 years and full privacy in 4-5. Smaller sizes take longer. Root Revive and TCP significantly speed this up.
What does my 1-year warranty cover?
Your warranty covers tree death due to disease, pests, or factors within our control during the first year after planting. It does not cover death due to drought stress if proper watering was not maintained, freeze damage from unusual weather events, or physical damage. When in doubt, call us — we'll always try to make it right.
When is the best time to prune my trees?
For most Texas trees, late winter (February-March) before new growth begins is ideal. This minimizes stress and reduces disease risk. Avoid heavy pruning in summer when trees are already heat-stressed. Light shaping can be done anytime. Our TCP visits include pruning assessments — we'll let you know if your trees need attention.
Can I plant trees in summer in Texas?
We don't recommend it unless absolutely necessary. Summer planting stresses trees significantly and requires much more intensive watering. Fall (October-November) and early spring (February-March) are the best times to plant in the Hill Country. If you must plant in summer, make sure you're committed to daily watering and have quality mulch around the base.
